04 / TENSIONS & PRINCIPLES

Good AI design was not about
maximising one side.

The system had to balance speed, trust,
automation and educational responsibility.

Industry ↓EDUCATOR↓ StudentStudent → Educator
Learning evidence

New industry knowledge is valuable only when educators can inspect and trust it.

→ Traceability by default

AI can accelerate work, but educational responsibility cannot disappear.

→ Human control at critical decisions

Industry knowledge must be adapted to the instructional context in which it will be used.

→ Context before generation

Conceptual balance, not a measured scale. Focus or select a tension to explore its design response.
